# Claim 3 pilot limitations

The pilot is calibration only and must remain BLOCKED regardless of attractive
numbers. Eight seeds are insufficient for the final uncertainty claim.

The reported paper table is one stochastic realization with no disclosed
seed. The final result can assess statistical compatibility but cannot prove
that it recovered the authors' exact RNG stream.

An empirical finite-K accuracy slope does not prove an asymptotic Theta claim.
The final report will distinguish the observed slope from Claim 2's exact
expected-advantage formula.
