# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

- The universal verdict rests on the symbolic posterior factorization. The
  finite exhaustive suite is corroboration, not the proof.
- The certificate covers `0 < x_i < 1`. At `x_i` equal to zero or one, the
  finite log-weight representation diverges; the Bayes decision is obtainable
  as a limit but is not certified here.
- Ties are treated as sets of all maximizers. The theorem determines the Bayes
  maximizing set; a particular randomized tie-breaking policy is outside the
  claim.
- The negative control deliberately violates conditional independence and
  therefore does not falsify the theorem. It demonstrates that the assumption
  is load-bearing.
